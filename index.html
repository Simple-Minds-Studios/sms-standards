Simple Minds Studios

The Studio’s Manual

Brand Bible · Version 0.1 · Working draft

This working manual defines how Simple Minds Studios should look, sound, move, and feel. It creates continuity across different forms of curiosity.

The studio may move between astronomy, human assessment, architecture, local history, grief, photography, artificial intelligence, infrastructure, fashion, and speculative design. The subject may change. The underlying presence should remain recognizable.

Dedicated chapters on colors, imagery, motion, typography, voice, museum metaphor and continuity are planned.
